# 📊 Vrinda Store Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Vrinda Store Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze e-commerce sales performance and customer purchasing behavior.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through **data cleaning, transformation, modeling, DAX calculations, KPIs, and interactive visualizations**.

It provides a consolidated view of sales performance based on **orders, sales amount, customer demographics, product categories, states, sales channels, and monthly trends**, helping users understand business performance and identify important sales patterns.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
* Track total sales amount and order volume.
* Identify monthly sales trends and seasonal patterns.
* Analyze sales performance across different states.
* Understand purchasing behavior based on customer gender.
* Compare sales across different age groups.
* Identify high-performing product categories.
* Analyze order quantity and sales contribution.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Build an interactive dashboard for business analysis.
* Present data in a clear and decision-oriented format.
